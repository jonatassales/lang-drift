# Integration brand assets

These assets identify their respective products; they are not LangDrift branding.

- `github-black.svg`, `github-white.svg`: unmodified Invertocat SVGs from the [GitHub Brand Toolkit](https://brand.github.com/foundations/logo), downloaded from its [logo archive](https://brand.github.com/GitHub_Logos.zip).
- `linear-dark.svg`, `linear-light.svg`: unmodified logo variants from [Linear Brand](https://linear.app/brand), downloaded from its [brand archive](https://static.linear.app/design-assets/Linear-Brand-Assets.zip?v=3).
- `obsidian.svg`: gradient logomark published under the [Obsidian Brand Guidelines](https://obsidian.md/brand), served from [Obsidian's site](https://obsidian.md/images/obsidian-logo-gradient.svg). The artwork is unmodified; only the root `width`/`height` were changed from `100%` to the `viewBox` dimensions so the file has intrinsic size outside Obsidian's own stylesheet. Obsidian asks to be contacted at support@obsidian.md for commercial use of its assets.

Brand colors, proportions and — where the brand publishes them — light/dark variants are preserved; every displayed mark also has its product name in HTML.
