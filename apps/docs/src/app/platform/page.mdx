# Platform

LangDrift is a platform of products, not one product. Each product stands on its own; together they share one account, one plan and one console.

## One domain

Everything is served under `https://langdrift.md` with a locale prefix: `/en/`, `/pt-br/`, `/zh-hant/`, `/ja/` and `/vi/`. The public site presents the platform and routes to each product's own page. Moving between products, on the site and in the console, is routing on that one domain, never a redirect to another domain or subdomain.

## One console

Each product has a management surface today, in its upstream repository:

| Product | Upstream surface |
| --- | --- |
| Vision | LangDrift's dashboard (`jonatassales/langdrift`, `apps/dashboard`): Overview, Vision, Evolution and Evidence workspaces, with attention, briefs, targets, revisions, drifts, decisions, relations and sources beneath them. |
| FinOps | Exphere's console (`jonatassales/exphere`, `apps/console`): projects registered from a local folder, their microruntimes, executions, history and secrets, supervised by one connected `ex` agent per project. |

They become **one LangDrift console**: one account, one shell, a **product switcher** between Vision and FinOps, and under it each product's own workspaces, sidebar and content. Both surfaces keep requested state apart from observed state and label illustrative or stub data as such; the unified console keeps both habits.

The console, the shell and the account are planned. Neither upstream console is in this repository yet.

## Principles the products share

- **The platform owns boundaries and mechanisms; the consumer owns the application and its domain choices.** A platform team may require observability, evidence, security and bounded execution without choosing every library.
- **Recommendation is not authorization.** An agent may estimate, recommend and execute permitted work. A deterministic control decides whether a paid or risky action is allowed. A model's suggestion never widens scope, funding or access.
- **The authorized budget belongs to the team**, not to a developer or a ticket. Allocations to work are subordinate to that envelope.
- **Unknown is never zero.** Missing or stale evidence is shown as missing, not as zero spend or a confirmed stop. Desired state and observed state stay distinct.
- **Evidence over assertion.** Every movement, recommendation and lifecycle operation keeps a token-free record with provenance. A claim of savings, delivery or readiness needs the evidence behind it.
- **Meaning is fixed across products.** Status, classification and drift colors never take a brand color, so a classification reads the same in every product.
