# Vision

**LangDrift Vision** keeps the why. A product moves away from the vision it set out with; Vision measures that movement and keeps the context of every move: what changed, who moved it, why, and whether a decision was recorded.

## Product Vision

**Product Vision** is the score that expresses how closely a product follows the vision it intended. The metric keeps its English name in every locale.

When the score moves, Vision keeps the movement's context in view:

- **What changed**, and its impact on the score.
- **Why it moved**, from the recorded product rationale.
- **Who was involved**: the people, teams and product areas behind the change.
- **The decision state**: whether a decision was recorded, and what still needs review.

The goal is shared understanding, not blame.

## Classifications

Every meaningful movement is classified:

| Classification | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Intentional evolution** | Movement backed by a recorded decision, reason and attributable actors. |
| **Unexplained drift** | Meaningful movement without enough recorded product rationale. |
| **Under review** | Evidence exists, but the relationship is not ready for a confident classification. |

Classification colors are fixed across the platform and never take a brand color, so a classification reads the same in every product.

## Drift is not a verdict

Drift describes movement relative to a product vision. It can reveal a gap, a trade-off, a realignment or a better direction. Evolution is expected; unexplained movement is different, and Vision makes that difference visible so a team can decide what to do about it.
