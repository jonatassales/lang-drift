# FinOps — SDD adaptation

Documentary draft. Follows the [common SDD cycle](../README.md) and its [definition](../definition.json) in full. No executor or integration has been implemented.

The [FIN guardrails](../../harness/guardrails/0001-finops.gr.md) and their [application rules](../../harness/rules/0002-finops.rule.md) are already local project policy. The checkpoints below remain a documentary adaptation of the cycle; execution mechanisms and organizational sources are still to be defined.

Adaptations to discuss with engineering, Cloud/FinOps, and finance:

- During discovery and specification: baseline, cost allocation, hypotheses, estimates, and economic and quality criteria.
- During preparation and execution: authorized decision, consumption assumptions, and expected change impact.
- During verification and observation: compare estimates against observed costs and consumption, accounting for volume, prices, and effects on the service.
- Artifacts and connections: optimization spec, estimates, decisions, and evidence; billing and consumption sources remain to be defined.

These checks can also be reused in other adaptations. Skills and checkpoints will be domain-specific while preserving common phases, contracts, and traceability.
