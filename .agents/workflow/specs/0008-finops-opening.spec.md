# FinOps opening: the envelope leads

**Status:** ready-for-agent

## Problem Statement

The FinOps product page opens in the Vision page's mold: a centered question headline with a gradient highlight, a lead, two calls to action, then the budget ledger. The ledger is the strongest material on the page, and it sits under a question. The receipt it drives lives two sections below, so selecting a work item changes something the visitor cannot see. The chain hides three scenarios behind tabs, and the within-envelope scenario retells in prose the figures the ledger already shows. The gate is three ordered cards without a figure on them, a weekly report card restates the ledger, and the early-access band closes the page with Vision's sentence about product decisions. A visitor who arrives from the Platform menu sees the same opening on the product pages and cannot tell, in the first twenty seconds, what FinOps does.

## Solution

The envelope leads. The page opens with a small mono eyebrow, "LangDrift FinOps", and a left-aligned statement as its `h1`: "One team envelope. Every paid call reserved before it runs." No gradient, no lead, no buttons. The budget ledger is the hero itself, with the delivery receipt docked beside it on wide screens and under it on narrow ones, so selecting a work item fills its receipt at once. The illustrative caption under the demo and the ledger's illustrative period stay. Then, in this order: the gate as one horizontal flow of three steps carrying the figures of the work item delivered inside the envelope (1,800 dollars proposed, 2,600 reserved, 2,460 observed, 140 released); the two failure scenarios, pause and replan and unknown after a timeout, side by side, each ending in a status line, with the weekly report's "needs attention" sentence folded into the pause scenario and the weekly report gone; envelope, allocation and reservation as a definition list; the sources as a plain list labelled as intended; the FAQ as it is; and the early-access band with a FinOps sentence of its own. Amounts stay simulated US dollars formatted for the locale, unknown stays unknown and never zero, and "recommendation is not authorization" stays visible as the gate's kicker. Once the FinOps page no longer renders `ProductHero`, nothing does, and it is deleted with its catalog keys.

## User Stories

1. As a visitor, I want the FinOps page to open with one statement instead of a question with a gradient, so that the opening reads differently from the Vision page.
2. As a visitor, I want the statement to name the envelope and the reservation before any paid call, so that the first line tells me what FinOps does.
3. As a visitor, I want the product named above the statement, so that I know which product page I am on without reading the header.
4. As a visitor, I want nothing but the eyebrow and the heading above the ledger, so that the demo is the opening and not an illustration under a paragraph.
5. As a visitor, I want the budget ledger to be the hero, so that I see the team envelope split into spent, reserved, protected and available at once.
6. As a visitor on a wide screen, I want the delivery receipt docked beside the ledger, so that selecting a work item shows its receipt without scrolling.
7. As a visitor on a narrow screen, I want the receipt under the ledger, so that both read in full at 375px with no horizontal overflow.
8. As a visitor, I want the receipt to follow the selected work item at once, so that the ledger and the receipt read as one instrument.
9. As a visitor, I want the caption that says the ledger is illustrative and the period that says the month is illustrative to stay, so that I never mistake the amounts for a measurement.
10. As a visitor, I want the work item with unknown spend to say unknown in the ledger and in the receipt, so that a missing charge never looks like zero.
11. As a visitor, I want the gate right after the hero as one horizontal flow of three steps, so that I read how a paid call is admitted in one line.
12. As a visitor, I want each step of the gate to carry a figure from the item delivered inside the envelope, 1,800 dollars proposed, 2,600 reserved, 2,460 observed and 140 released, so that the gate shows amounts moving instead of describing them.
13. As a visitor, I want the gate to say which receipt its figures come from, so that the hero and the gate read as one example.
14. As a visitor, I want the gate's kicker to say that recommendation is not authorization, so that I know a model never widens funding, scope or access.
15. As a visitor, I want the gate's lead to say it is the intended design and not a shipped control, so that the page claims nothing it cannot show.
16. As a visitor, I want the two failure scenarios side by side instead of behind tabs, so that I compare a pause with an unknown without clicking.
17. As a visitor, I want each scenario to walk intent, delivery, evidence and outcome in the same order, so that the chain reads the same in both.
18. As a visitor, I want the pause scenario to end with the sentence that says the reindexing job is paused and a decision is pending, so that the weekly report's warning survives without the report.
19. As a visitor, I want the unknown scenario to end with the observed spend shown as unknown and awaiting reconciliation, so that it ends in the state the ledger shows.
20. As a visitor, I want the within-envelope scenario gone from the scenarios, so that the page does not tell in prose what the hero's receipt and the gate already show in figures.
21. As a visitor, I want envelope, allocation and reservation as a definition list, so that I read three definitions instead of three cards competing for attention.
22. As a visitor, I want the sources as a plain list labelled as intended, so that I know what would feed the ledger and that none of it is connected yet.
23. As a visitor, I want the FAQ as it is, so that the questions I would ask in a demo are still answered on the page.
24. As a visitor, I want the early-access band to speak about FinOps, so that the page does not close with a sentence about product decisions.
25. As a visitor, I want the weekly report section gone, so that the page does not restate the ledger it opened with.
26. As a visitor reading pt-BR, zh-Hant, ja or vi, I want the whole page in my language with the same structure and the same illustrative amounts formatted for my locale, so that nothing depends on English.
27. As a visitor in either color scheme, I want the page to read correctly in light and dark, so that the theme toggle never breaks a section.
28. As a keyboard user, I want the ledger's work items reachable with Tab and operable with Enter and Space, with the receipt announced when it changes, so that the hero works without a pointer.
29. As a screen reader user, I want the hero's summary to describe the ledger, the receipt beside it and the simulated amounts, so that I understand the hero without the layout.
30. As a search engine, I want the page's title, description, canonical, hreflang, social images and sitemap entry unchanged, so that nothing re-indexes for a redesign.
31. As a frontend developer, I want the ledger and the receipt to keep sharing one atom, so that the docked receipt needs no new wiring.
32. As a frontend developer, I want the gate's figures derived from the work item the ledger selects first, so that the hero and the gate never disagree.
33. As a frontend developer, I want the early-access band to take the product's sentence from the page, so that each product page can close with its own copy without a second band.
34. As a frontend developer, I want every new or changed string in the JSON catalog with parity across the five locales, so that the localization checks stay green.
35. As a frontend developer, I want `ProductHero` deleted with its catalog keys once nothing renders it, so that the dead-code check stays green and no page inherits the Vision mold by default.
36. As the owner, I want the copy drafted in all five locales, so that I edit the catalogs rather than start from nothing.
37. As the owner, I want the Vision and Microruntimes pages untouched, so that this change restyles no sibling.

## Implementation Decisions

- **Opening.** The FinOps page composes its own hero section instead of rendering `ProductHero`, as the Microruntimes page does under spec 0007: a landmark labelled by a left-aligned `h1` in the product pages' heading classes that reads "One team envelope. Every paid call reserved before it runs.", each sentence on its own line as the home hero sets its two lines, with a mono eyebrow "LangDrift FinOps" above it, no `ChromaText`, no lead and no calls to action. Under it, a grid wired by `aria-describedby` to the visible caption and a screen-reader summary: the budget ledger in a first column of three fifths and the delivery receipt in a second column of two fifths from the `lg` breakpoint up, stacked below it. The caption keeps the existing illustrative sentence and sits under the grid, left-aligned. The summary describes the envelope, the four work items, the unknown item and the receipt beside the ledger with its rows, and says the amounts are simulated. The skip link keeps its text and points at the gate, the first section after the hero.
- **Ledger.** `BudgetLedgerDemo` fills its grid column: it loses its own centering and maximum width. On narrow screens each amount's label sits on its own line above the amount, because the site's buttons do not wrap their text and a long currency format such as pt-BR's would otherwise run into the next column at 375px. Nothing else changes. Its label, period ("This month · illustrative"), authorized amount, stacked bar, legend, columns, work items and select hint stay.
- **Receipt.** `DeliveryReceipt` is the hero's second column. It loses the top margin it had as a section body; its heading becomes an `h2`, since it now sits directly under the `h1`; its head stacks the selected item's eyebrow, title and owner over the observed spend at every width, so the card reads the same docked at two fifths and stacked at full width. The live region, the `DetailList` rows (estimate, reserved, model tier and effort, retries, verification, cleanup, evidence) and the unknown-spend rendering in the warning tone stay. The receipt section and its kicker, title and lead are removed.
- **Gate flow.** A new `GateFlow` Server Component in the FinOps components replaces the three ordered cards: an ordered list of three steps that sit in one row from the `lg` breakpoint, with an arrow between steps as the authoring flow draws it, and stack below it with the arrows turned. Each step keeps the existing eyebrow ("01 · The agent recommends", "02 · The gate reserves", "03 · The evidence closes"), title and description, and carries a mono figure with a label: proposed 1,800 dollars on the first step, reserved 2,600 on the second, observed 2,460 on the third with a second figure of 140 dollars released to the envelope. A line under the flow names the receipt the figures come from, as an ICU message with the work item's title, and says they are illustrative. The section keeps its kicker ("Recommendation is not authorization"), title, lead and fragment id, and moves up to sit right after the hero.
- **Figures from the content module.** The content module exports the gate's example, the first work item, the one the ledger selects first, and a pure function that derives the gate figures from a work item with observed spend: proposed is its estimate, reserved its reservation, observed its observed spend and released the difference between the two. The page formats them with the module's dollar formatter for the locale; no amount is typed into copy or markup.
- **Failure scenarios.** A new `FailureScenarios` Server Component replaces `PillarScenarios` on this page: two articles side by side from the `lg` breakpoint, stacked below it, one for pause and replan and one for unknown after a timeout. Each has the scenario's title, its context line and a `DetailList` whose rows are the four pillars, intent, delivery, evidence and outcome, with the existing texts, closed by a status row: the pause scenario's reads "Needs attention" with the weekly report's sentence in the danger tone; the unknown scenario's reads "Observed spend" with "Unknown · awaiting reconciliation" in the warning tone. The within-envelope scenario leaves the catalog with the tab labels, the selector label and the chain outro; its case lives in the hero's receipt and in the gate. The section takes the fragment id `failures`, the old chain title ("Intent → Delivery → Evidence → Outcome") as its kicker, a title that says the envelope holds when the plan does not, and a lead that names the two failures and points at the receipt above for the case that lands inside the envelope. The status tones live in the content module beside the scenario ids.
- **Definitions.** The budget section keeps its kicker, title and lead and renders envelope, allocation and reservation as a `DetailList`: the term is the former card's eyebrow; the detail is the former card's title, set stronger in the foreground color, over its description. No card.
- **Sources.** A new `SourceList` Server Component renders the four sources as a plain list, two columns from the `md` breakpoint, under a mono label "Intended sources": each item's title set stronger over its description, the Microruntimes item keeping its link to the Microruntimes page. The sentence that says these are the sources the model intends closes the list. The section keeps its kicker, title and lead.
- **FAQ.** Unchanged.
- **Early-access band.** `EarlyAccessSection` gains an optional description. When the page passes one, the landing band shows it under its heading instead of the shared sentence; without one, the shared sentence stays, so the Vision and Microruntimes pages render as before. The FinOps page passes a sentence from its own catalog that invites the reader to see what a team envelope, a reservation and a receipt would look like for their own work, and what FinOps does not promise.
- **Removed.** `ProductHero` and its copy interface are deleted: after this change nothing renders them. `PillarScenarios`, `SectionOutro`, `RecordCard` and `InfoCards` leave the FinOps page but stay, since the Microruntimes page and its authoring flow render them. `ChromaText` stays for the Vision page. `selectedWorkItemAtom` is unchanged; the ledger writes it and the receipt reads it, as before.
- **Copy and data.** The FinOps catalog, five locales in one file in the same key order, changes as follows. `hero` becomes an eyebrow, the title's two lines (a start and an end), the caption and the summary. `receipt` loses its kicker, title and lead. `chain` becomes `failures`: a kicker, a title, a lead, the four pillar labels and two scenarios, each with a title, a context, the four pillar texts and a status term and detail. `authorization` keeps its kicker, title and lead, renames its cards to steps that each gain a figure label, the third also a released label, and gains the source line as an ICU message with the item's title. `budget` renames its cards to terms, each with a term, a title and a description. `report` is removed; its attention sentence moves into the pause scenario's status. `sources` gains the "Intended sources" label. A new `earlyAccess` family holds the band's description. The product name, "LangDrift FinOps", stays untranslated; amounts never appear in copy. The content module renames the card id lists to the step and term ids they now describe, replaces the chain scenario ids with the two failure ids and their status tones, adds the gate example and the figures function, and retypes the catalog shape from the id arrays.
- **Metadata and routes.** Unchanged. The page keeps its title, description, canonical, hreflang, social images and sitemap entries. Fragment ids on the page: `authorization`, `failures`, `budget`, `sources`, `faq`, `early-access`.
- **Workflow.** One commit per ticket on `feat/finops-opening` with its `Ticket:` trailer and no AI attribution lines, then a pull request into `main`.

## Testing Decisions

A good test checks what a visitor or a build sees: rendered text, ARIA state, the store value after a click, a catalog's parity. It never asserts class names or internal structure.

Seams, highest first:

1. **Catalog parity** (existing seam): the FinOps catalog stays in the family list of the website's catalog test, which checks keys, ICU arguments and rich tags against `en` and formats every message with counts 0, 1, 2, 3 and 12 in every locale. The catalog index's shape assertion covers key order.
2. **Content module** (existing seam, new assertions): the gate example is the work item the ledger selects first and has observed spend; its gate figures are 1,800 proposed, 2,600 reserved, 2,460 observed and 140 released; the failure scenario ids leave the within-envelope case out; every failure scenario has a status tone.
3. **Hero demo** (existing seam): the jsdom test of the ledger and the receipt in a fresh Jotai store keeps its cases, selecting an item puts its id in the store and shows its receipt, the unknown item shows the warning copy and never `0`, the model tier and the retries format through the catalog, with the receipt's heading now a level-two heading.
4. **Gate flow and failure scenarios** (new tests at an existing kind of seam): jsdom tests like the early-access section's render the two Server Components with the English catalog and assert what a visitor reads: the three step eyebrows in order with the four formatted amounts; the two scenario titles, the pause scenario ending in the needs-attention sentence, the unknown scenario ending in the unknown copy, and no `$0` anywhere.
5. **Early-access band** (existing seam): the section's jsdom test gains a case: with a description from the page the landing band shows it and not the shared sentence; without one, the shared sentence.
6. **Repository checks**: `pnpm turbo run check`, `pnpm turbo run typecheck test --filter=website --filter=@langdrift/web`, `pnpm turbo run build --filter=website`, `drift check localization`, `drift check dead-code`.

Manual verification in the built-in browser: `/en/platform/finops/` and `/pt-br/platform/finops/` at desktop width and at 375px, in light and dark, with no horizontal overflow; the receipt following the selected work item beside the ledger and under it; the gate's four amounts; the two scenario columns; the definitions, the sources, the FAQ and the band's FinOps sentence; the Vision and Microruntimes pages unchanged.

## Out of Scope

- The Vision and Microruntimes pages, and the shared `PillarScenarios`, `SectionOutro`, `RecordCard` and `InfoCards`, which stay for the Microruntimes page.
- Reopening sign-up in the early-access band.
- A real gate, ledger, reservation or any console feature; the page describes the intended model with illustrative values.
- Metadata, routes and the sitemap.

## Further Notes

The direction was audited with the owner before this spec and confirmed in one grilling round: the envelope leads, the product name sits as an eyebrow over the statement, there is no lead, the receipt docks beside the ledger, the gate carries the within-envelope figures, the failure scenarios stand side by side, the report goes, definitions and sources become lists and the band speaks for the product. Spec 0007 is the precedent for a product page that composes its own opening; spec 0005 remains the source for what the FinOps page shows and for the maturity its claims may have.
