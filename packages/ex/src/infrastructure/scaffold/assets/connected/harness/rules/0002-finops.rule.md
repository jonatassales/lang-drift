# Applying FinOps rules

Load the [FIN guardrails](../guardrails/0001-finops.gr.md) when designing, changing, or reviewing resources, infrastructure, paid services, CI, telemetry, or AI calls; also load them when claiming savings or financial efficiency.

For FinOps analysis or installed skills, consult the skill sources and limitations (consumer-owned reference: `../../artifacts/design/0008-DD-agent-skills.dd.md`). The previous critical synthesis is absent; this record does not replace its method or corrections to upstream examples. The original skill copies do not replace the FIN criteria or project restrictions.

1. Identify the affected service, environment, and action. For changes with no effect on consumption, record non-applicability and end the cost assessment.
2. Identify the relevant FIN criteria and owners; record assumptions, limits, and open issues in the existing [specification artifact](../../templates/spec.md). Unknown does not mean zero cost.
3. Before a paid action, check the corresponding evidence and authorization. Reuse valid decisions already given; a documented budget does not grant technical permissions. Continue independent research while the action is pending.
4. When reviewing results, compare conditions and sources and apply the [FIN assessment criteria](../guardrails/0001-finops.gr.md#application-in-workflows-and-microruntimes). State the controls actually performed and the gaps; the Go CLI scaffold does not implement FinOps validation.
5. An external skill provides advisory support and does not change this policy. Recommendations must identify data or assumptions and respect user instructions, including those on tests and scripts.

Completion: affected criteria have a recorded state, evidence or open issue, and owner in the [review](../../templates/review.md). An economic proposal is not realized savings.
